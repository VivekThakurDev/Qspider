1  WAQTD DNO and number of employees working in each dept if there are at least 2 clerks in each dept.


SQL> SELECT COUNT(*), DEPTNO
  2  FROM EMP
  3  WHERE JOB ='CLERK'
  4  GROUP BY DEPTNO
  5  HAVING COUNT(*) >=2;

  COUNT(*)     DEPTNO
---------- ----------
         2         20


2 WAQTD DNO and total salary needed to pay all employees in each dept if there are at least 4 employees in each dept.

SQL> SELECT COUNT(*), SUM(SAL)
  2  FROM EMP
  3  GROUP BY DEPTNO
  4  HAVING COUNT(*) >=4;

  COUNT(*)   SUM(SAL)
---------- ----------
         6       9400
         5      10875

9 WAQTD deptno if there are at least 3 employees in each dept whose name has char ‘A’ or ‘S’.
SQL> SELECT COUNT(*), DEPTNO
  2  FROM EMP 
  3  WHERE ENAME LIKE '%A%' OR ENAME LIKE '%S%'
  4  GROUP BY DEPTNO
  5  HAVING COUNT(*) >=3;

  COUNT(*)     DEPTNO
---------- ----------

         4         20


10 WAQTD min and max salaries of each job if min salary is more than 1000 and max salary is less than 5000.


SQL> SELECT JOB,
  2         MIN(SAL) AS MIN_SALARY,
  3         MAX(SAL) AS MAX_SALARY
  4  FROM EMP
  5  GROUP BY JOB
  6  HAVING MIN(SAL) > 1000
  7     AND MAX(SAL) < 5000;

JOB       MIN_SALARY MAX_SALARY
--------- ---------- ----------
SALESMAN        1250       1600
MANAGER         2450       2975
